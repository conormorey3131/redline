Who We Are

Redline Events is an events company based in Killarney, specialising in sports and corporate events across Ireland.

From cycling and endurance events to corporate activations and experience days, we focus on practical planning, smooth operations and professional delivery.

Current projects include the Killarney Classic Tour alongside a growing range of partnerships and event productions built around detail, experience and reliability.

What We Specialise In

  • Corporate Events

    Team experiences, brand activations and hospitality days designed to engage clients and energise teams.

  • Cycling Events

    Sportives, gran fondos and charity rides delivered with professional logistics and a premium rider experience.

  • Event Management

    Full-service delivery — logistics, operations, marketing and on-the-day execution. We handle every detail.

  • Adventure Racing

    Multi-discipline endurance events that push limits and bring out the best in every competitor.
